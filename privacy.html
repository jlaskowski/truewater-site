Truewater Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Truewater: Pool & Hot Tub Care ("Truewater") is made by Jim Laskowski. This page explains what happens to your information. The short version: Truewater doesn't collect any of your data.

What stays on your device

Your pools and hot tubs, water tests, the chemicals you log, and your settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. There's no account, no sign-in and no server of ours.

iCloud sync (Truewater Pro)

If you have Truewater Pro and leave iCloud sync on, your data is also stored in your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, so it's the same on all your devices. Only you can read it; we can't. You can turn sync off in Truewater's Settings, and delete the iCloud copy in your device's Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Purchases

Truewater Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don't see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Reminders

Test reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Truewater shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising code, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Exporting

If you export your tests (a CSV file), the file goes wherever you choose to share or save it.

Children

Truewater doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
