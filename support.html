Getting started

Add your pool or hot tub with its size (Truewater can work it out from the shape), then tap New test and enter the numbers from your test strips or drop kit. You can leave out any reading you didn't test. Truewater shows what to add, how much, and why, in order.

Common questions

The amount seems large. Check the size of your pool and the strength on your chemical's label (for example 10% or 12.5% liquid chlorine) under Edit. Big changes are split in half, with a retest in between.

Why is my chlorine target higher than the label says? Stabilizer (CYA) protects chlorine from the sun but also slows it down, so the more CYA you have, the more chlorine you need. Truewater uses the FC/CYA method.

I bought Pro on another device. Open Settings in Truewater and tap Restore purchases.

My pools aren't syncing. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on both devices with the same Apple ID, and that iCloud sync is on in Truewater's Settings.

Reminders don't arrive. Allow notifications for Truewater in your device's Settings > Notifications.

Safety

Never mix acid and chlorine, or different chlorine products. Add chemicals to water, never water to chemicals. Keep hot tubs at 104°F or below. Truewater gives general guidance, not professional advice: always follow the product label. If someone swallows a pool chemical or gets it in their eyes, call Poison Control (1-800-222-1222 in the US) or emergency services.
